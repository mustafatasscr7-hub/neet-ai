A
What is the difference between C3 and C4 plants in photosynthesis?
N
C3 plants fix CO₂ directly in the Calvin cycle using RuBisCO — first stable product is 3-PGA, a 3-carbon compound. They photorespire heavily in hot, dry conditions.

C4 plants fix CO₂ first in mesophyll cells (4-carbon oxaloacetate), then concentrate it in bundle sheath cells — Kranz anatomy. Sugarcane, maize, sorghum are C4. Wheat, rice, most trees are C3.
