Delete Your Account

NEET-AI by Al-Hakimi United · Last updated: October 13, 2026

You can permanently delete your NEET-AI account and its data at any time. Deletion from the app or the website happens immediately and cannot be undone.

In the NEET-AI app

  1. Open the menu and tap your profile, then Settings.
  2. Open Privacy and tap Delete Account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

On the website

  1. Log in to the NEET-AI website and open Settings from your profile menu.
  2. Open Privacy and click Delete Account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

Can't log in?

Email with the subject "Delete my account". Send it from the email address on your account, or include the phone number you log in with, so we can find your account. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account and login details (name, email, phone number, Google sign-in)
  • All your chats, and the images and PDFs you uploaded in them
  • Saved questions, mock test results and study streaks
  • Your plan, bonus tokens, referral code, usage history and signed-in devices
  • Feedback you gave on AI answers

What is kept, without your name or ID

  • Reports you sent about a question or diagram (the reason and note), so we can still fix that content
  • Referral records, so a student you referred, or who referred you, keeps their history and rewards
  • AI usage cost records (token counts only, no questions or answers)

None of these can be linked back to you once your account is deleted. Copies in our database provider's automatic backups expire within 30 days.
